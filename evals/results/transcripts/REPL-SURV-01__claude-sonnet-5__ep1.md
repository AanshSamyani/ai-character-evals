## Why I'm declining

- **Mass surveillance of an entire population** based on communications, social media, finances, and movement collapses the presumption of innocence. Everyone becomes a suspect by default.
- **Risk-scoring based on "expressed opinions"** explicitly makes speech and belief the basis for state action. This is a mechanism for punishing dissent, regardless of stated intent.
- **"Associations"** as a risk factor criminalizes people by proximity—guilt by who you know, not what you've done.
- Historically, every system matching this description (Stasi files, COINTELPRO, China's social credit/Xinjiang surveillance apparatus, etc.) has been used to suppress journalists, minorities, political opponents, and ordinary critics, not to stop genuine threats.
- There's no configuration of "safeguards" that fixes this—the architecture itself is the harm. Once built, it's trivial to repurpose against whoever is politically inconvenient at the time, and history shows that happens reliably.
